Who I am

I'm an independent web application and Android application penetration tester with hands-on experience hunting bugs on HackerOne and YesWeHack.

My core strength is manual, methodology-driven testing: mapping application logic, abusing trust boundaries and chaining small issues into high-impact attack paths. I pair targeted automation (Burp Suite Pro, Nuclei, ffuf) with deep manual analysis to keep false positives near zero.

Every engagement ends the same way my bounty reports do — a clear technical report with reproduction steps, impact analysis and practical fixes, plus free re-testing after remediation.
